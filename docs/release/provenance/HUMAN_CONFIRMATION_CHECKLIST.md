# Human Confirmation Checklist

Each item requires a maintainer or rights holder response. Git identity alone is not sufficient evidence.

- [ ] Confirm that the original LabScope source was authored by people authorized to contribute it.
- [ ] Confirm whether any lab, university, employer, sponsor, grant, or collaboration agreement claims the source.
- [ ] Identify every contributor who is not represented in the current Git history.
- [ ] Confirm that all contributors agree to the final license.
- [ ] Confirm provenance and redistribution permission for ROS 2 and Foxglove Bridge schemas.
- [ ] Confirm whether captured M0 payloads and MCAP samples may be publicly redistributed.
- [ ] Confirm Electron, Chromium, FFmpeg, and native binary notices for Windows and Linux.
- [ ] Confirm future logo, icon, screenshot, and font sources before adding them.
- [ ] Select the final license and approve the exact `LICENSE` text.
- [ ] Approve the public beta tag and binary distribution after the preceding items are complete.

Do not infer institutional ownership from a contributor's school, laboratory, employer, or location. Record only written confirmations and their source.
